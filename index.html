Shift coverage for restaurants

Every shift covered, by text.

When someone calls in sick, Covered texts the staff who can work the shift, in the order you prefer. You run the schedule by chatting with Covered. Your staff just text.

Talk to us See how it works

Less time on the phone, fewer empty shifts

  • Sick calls covered in minutes

    Someone can't make it? Covered texts the staff who can work that shift, in the order you prefer, and tells you when it's filled. No group texts, no phone tree.

  • Your schedule, in a conversation

    Ask "Who can cover Sunday?", "Draft next week's FOH" or "Give Ana Friday off". Covered reads the schedule, does the work and asks before anything goes out.

  • Nothing for staff to install

    Offers, published schedules and pickups arrive by text. Staff reply to call in sick, ask for time off or grab an open shift, and each has their own schedule page.

  • Your rules, every time

    Covered knows who's certified for each role, who works FOH or BOH, your standard week and who's off, and warns you before anyone is double-booked.

How it works

  1. 1

    Set up once

    Your roles, shifts and standard week, imported from the schedule you already keep.

  2. 2

    Draft and publish each week

    Covered drafts the week from your standard week. You adjust it in the chat, then publish, and each person gets their shifts by text.

  3. 3

    Let Covered handle changes

    Sick calls, swaps and open shifts are handled by text, with a heads-up to you, and the schedule stays current.
