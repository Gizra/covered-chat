About Covered

Covered started with a restaurant's real problem: a shift opens at the last minute, and the manager spends the next hour texting around to fill it. Covered does that texting for them, and lets them run the rest of the schedule by simply asking.

It's made by a small team at Gizra USA, LLC, the people behind ManyRoads.
