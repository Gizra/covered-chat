# Shift coverage by text, for restaurants

**Covered** helps a restaurant keep every shift covered. It is a product of **Gizra USA, LLC**.

When someone calls in sick or can't make a shift, Covered texts the staff who can work it, in the order the restaurant prefers. The first person to reply **YES** gets the shift, and the manager is told.

## How it works

- **Owners and managers** run the schedule by chatting with Covered: drafting and publishing the week, filling open shifts, and approving time off.
- **Staff** text Covered to call in sick, ask for time off, or pick up an open shift, and get their schedule when it's published.
- **Texts are about work shifts only**, and go only to employees who agreed to receive them on the restaurant's [SMS consent form](/sms-consent/). Reply STOP to any message to opt out, or HELP for help.

## Who runs Covered

Covered is built and operated by [Gizra USA, LLC](https://www.gizra.com). Restaurants use it to schedule their own staff; it sends no marketing or promotional messages.
