## 1. Program

The Covered SMS program is run by Gizra USA, LLC. Restaurants use Covered to coordinate work shifts with their staff by text message. By opting in, you agree to receive text messages from Covered about your work shifts on behalf of the restaurant you work for: offers of open shifts (reply **YES** to take one), your published schedule, notices when you're covering a coworker's shift, and replies to texts you send Covered (for example to call in sick or ask for time off). Covered does not send marketing or promotional messages.

## 2. How you opt in

You opt in by signing the [shift text message consent form](/sms-consent/) when you give your mobile number to the restaurant you work for. Agreeing to receive texts is not a condition of employment.
